IEEE Publication & Research

IEEE i3ctcon 2026 Published
March 14, 2026

Unpaired Adverse-Weather Image Translation Using GAN Architectures with XAI-Driven Interpretability Across Multi-Domain Conditions

Authors: Skanda R. Bharadwaja & 5 Co-authors Mentors: Prof Ashwini, Prof Merin Thomas
Deep Learning Generative Adversarial Networks Explainable AI (XAI) Image-to-Image Translation Computer Vision

Research Abstract & Framework

This research introduces a novel deep learning framework designed to translate autonomous driving camera feeds between multiple adverse weather conditions (Rain, Heavy Fog, Snow) using unpaired Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs). We deployed CycleGAN and FastCUT architectures with a ResNet-based generator enhanced by VGG Perceptual loss. To interpret model decision-making across weather domains, Explainable AI (XAI) techniques (Grad-CAM & LIME) were integrated, providing spatial heatmaps of generator focus regions. Performance was validated with quantitative benchmarks: FID, PSNR, and SSIM.
